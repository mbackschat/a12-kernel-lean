#### Repeatable validation

- `dynamic-key-view`: [Selected-field observation](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/CheckedStarDocument.lean) reads dynamic validation keys through the existing full-validation coordinate domain, then applies preliminary defaults and findings. An implicit parent supplies an empty key without creating stored rows. Computation keeps its physical read, and missing/out-of-domain or malformed bindings retain exact errors.
- `dynamic-key-assurance`: [Reader and direct-source controls](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/SelectedMessageLocks.lean) cover Number, String, Boolean and Date payloads, numeric OR order/zero-empty behavior, admitted guarded presence, physical positive twins and phase/domain/binding refusals. The [whole-cache](../sources/message-and-pointer-probes.md#src-message-cache-population-order) and [JSON conversion](../sources/evaluation-and-application-routes.md#src-json-row-normalization) captures retain the full-rule absent-parent discriminator.

- `selected-parent-admission`: Boolean/Confirm value counts and Number, token and temporal uniqueness readers pass the enclosing rule binding into the existing entity-shape resolver. Direct companions retain parent scopes; selected operands retain their own lookup scope. [source](../sources/group-list-and-capacity-probes.md#src-walk-batch-reading-scope) calibrates six carrier/constant rows in both operand orders with literal and dynamic keys. Evaluation, computation-target gates and Boolean filter certificates are unchanged; other temporal/token profiles are not calibrated by this matrix.
- `selected-guards`: The shared Number, token and Boolean entity guard classifier retains ordinary field scopes and selected operands' enclosing parent plus dynamic-key scopes. Selected products retain each side independently. Existing zero/positive literal sensitivity is unchanged; mixed-reference rejection is operator-specific and excludes `SumOfProducts`, matching the source visitor.
- `selected-guard-assurance`: [Reader/assembly locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/SelectedIterationLocks.lean) separate both disjunction orders, conjunction rescue, literal and dynamic selection, group/lower-star sources, bound ordinary companions, zero/positive/mixed refusals, and independent key-level versus parent-level legality. The [source](../sources/message-and-pointer-probes.md#src-selected-message-reference-resolution) supplies two admitted Sum disjunctions; wider carrier behavior is source-based reuse with external evidence pending. The ordinary Boolean companion is locked through checked-source assembly and the reader. Restoring the erased Boolean field scope fails that lock. The existing per-level guard algebra and laws are reused.
- `ordinary-guard`: [The guard](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationCondition/Iteration.lean) skips a level whose group repeats exactly twice and declares an index field, and judges a comparison leaf by three constant rules, a count below a positive constant, a value operation equal to a constant converting to zero and mixed iterating and fixed reads, with `CurrentRepetition` unguarded and the value-list quantifiers judged by where their subjects and values read. [Assembly](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationRule.lean) answers `errorFieldNotReferenced` ahead of the guard. A direct Number aggregate list naming a row field is admitted on the [addressed comparison carrier](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/NumericValidation/Ordered.lean), which reads it at the rule's row (`external evidence pending`: the K04 capture is static only). A `$` read inside a filter does not reference a level for any leaf. The flat model does not mark H models, whose exemption needs no index field.
- `ordinary-guard-selections`: A field or group selection weighs one slot per row below its selected row in field-fill and uniqueness lists, never its selected level, and its presence leaf references its parent's and key's levels; the [reader](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/LowerValidation.lean) binds a singleton field or group selection at the error field's rows.
- `ordinary-guard-assurance`: The [reader lock](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/IterationGuardLocks.lean) holds the [K04 checkpoint](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-iteration-guard-k04)'s rule-check rows and the [peer's](../sources/inbound-handbacks.md#src-inbound-2026-10-22b) constant rows, holds three source-read filter rows, the [binding lock](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/IterationBindingLocks.lean) holds the [binding checkpoint](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-iteration-binding-k04)'s selection rows, and the locks separate a per-row direct `Sum`, `MaxValue` and `NumberOfDifferentValues` from a fixed-row read and a cross-row sum. The singleton legality laws carry the skipped level as a hypothesis.
- `group-error-target`: [Assembly](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationRule.lean) admits a group as the error entity through its [placement](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationRule/ErrorPlacement.lean): the levels above the group iterate and need a binding reference, the group's own level is pinned to row 1 for field and subgroup reads while the group's own presence reads every row, and only that presence or a containing group's presence references the target. The guard still judges the group's own level. The rule's own group is refused with no class ahead of every other gate; below the root the root is an ordinary group. Flat, repeatable field presence, group presence and ordered numeric leaves are admitted, those beyond field and group presence and a starred `Sum` reusing the first-row read `external evidence pending`. A root-only reference, another leaf, the level and scope gates and partial validation (ahead of the filter skip) refuse without a Kernel class.
- `group-error-target-assurance`: The [group-target lock](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/GroupTargetLocks.lean) holds the [runtime checkpoint](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-group-target-nested-join)'s admission rows and three of the [error-target path inbound](../sources/inbound-handbacks.md#inbound-2026-09-26m-error-target-paths)'s rows, the [guard lock](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/IterationGuardLocks.lean) the checkpoint's guard rows, and `lake exe a12-kernel-replay` its pointers and references. The [reader](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/Validate.lean) reads the error-target path lexically from the rule element, the lock holding the inbound's `..` spellings, and a computation target naming a group refuses without a class. The blank-root rows remain a [returned omission](../PLAN.md#k19-returned-omissions). The negative-iteration refusal law is restated over every error entity; no law covers the released group-presence read.

- `boundary`: Ordinary full/partial rule loops over concrete outer rows plus recursive implicit nested child row 1; partial rule admission projects error-path relevance onto the levels bound by the checked reference-derived iteration plan and ignores deeper coordinates, while admitted ordinary field and Number-aggregate reads consume the call-local generated-preliminary view, including requiredness, relevance-scoped duplicate-index findings, and cause-free default suppression
- `owner`: [`ValidationRule.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationRule.lean) assembly and [`ValidationRule/Execution.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationRule/Execution.lean) execution, [`ValidationCondition/Iteration.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationCondition/Iteration.lean), [partial-preliminary projection law](../../A12Kernel/Proofs/CheckedIndexPreliminary.lean)
- `assurance`: E/P closed for admitted loops, ordinary field leaves, and unfiltered Number aggregates, including three-level projection, empty-as-zero full/partial execution, no-outer control, nonmaterialization, row-exact admission, relevance-scoped duplicate suppression, requiredness, and a zero-bound once plan admitted by another concrete error row; upstream L locked at a12-dmkits [source registry](../SOURCES.md) for the row-domain and iteration-bound gate splits and [source registry](../SOURCES.md) for duplicate-partner relevance; selected C/X
- `remains`: Wider leaves/parallel shapes: [SG9](../SEMANTICS-GAPS.md#sg9--paths-indices-and-static-legality-completion)

- `parallel-partial-assurance`: [Reader locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/PartialParallelRuleLocks.lean) cover six comparisons, seven fill operators, field/group presence, dispatch unions, wildcard-versus-concrete availability, Number aliases, duplicate cache population and frame bounds 3/6. - `parallel-instance-leaves`: A leaf with no pair form is lowered by the ordinary reader against the [instance view](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ParallelInstanceView.lean), which makes the joined groups fixed, and evaluates as a model-free flat leaf over the joined row; an unmatched partner reads empty.
