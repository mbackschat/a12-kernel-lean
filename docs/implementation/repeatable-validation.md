#### Repeatable validation

- `dynamic-key-view`: [Selected-field observation](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/CheckedStarDocument.lean) reads dynamic validation keys through the existing full-validation coordinate domain, then applies preliminary defaults and findings. An implicit parent supplies an empty key without creating stored rows. Computation keeps its physical read, and missing/out-of-domain or malformed bindings retain exact errors.
- `dynamic-key-assurance`: [Reader and direct-source controls](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/SelectedMessageLocks.lean) cover Number, String, Boolean and Date payloads, numeric OR order/zero-empty behavior, admitted guarded presence, physical positive twins and phase/domain/binding refusals. The [whole-cache](../sources/message-and-pointer-probes.md#src-message-cache-population-order) and [JSON conversion](../sources/evaluation-and-application-routes.md#src-json-row-normalization) captures retain the full-rule absent-parent discriminator.

- `selected-parent-admission`: Boolean/Confirm value counts and Number, token and temporal uniqueness readers pass the enclosing rule binding into the existing entity-shape resolver. Direct companions retain parent scopes; selected operands retain their own lookup scope. [source](../sources/group-list-and-capacity-probes.md#src-walk-batch-reading-scope) calibrates six carrier/constant rows in both operand orders with literal and dynamic keys. Evaluation, computation-target gates and Boolean filter certificates are unchanged; other temporal/token profiles are not calibrated by this matrix.
- `selected-guards`: The shared Number, token and Boolean entity guard classifier retains ordinary field scopes and selected operands' enclosing parent plus dynamic-key scopes. Selected products retain each side independently. Existing zero/positive literal sensitivity is unchanged; mixed-reference rejection is operator-specific and excludes `SumOfProducts`, matching the source visitor.
- `selected-guard-assurance`: [Reader/assembly locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/SelectedIterationLocks.lean) separate both disjunction orders, conjunction rescue, literal and dynamic selection, group/lower-star sources, bound ordinary companions, zero/positive/mixed refusals, and independent key-level versus parent-level legality. The [source](../sources/message-and-pointer-probes.md#src-selected-message-reference-resolution) supplies two admitted Sum disjunctions; wider carrier behavior is source-based reuse with external evidence pending. The ordinary Boolean companion is locked through checked-source assembly and the reader. Restoring the erased Boolean field scope fails that lock. The existing per-level guard algebra and laws are reused.

- `boundary`: Ordinary full/partial rule loops over concrete outer rows plus recursive implicit nested child row 1; partial rule admission projects error-path relevance onto the levels bound by the checked reference-derived iteration plan and ignores deeper coordinates, while admitted ordinary field and Number-aggregate reads consume the call-local generated-preliminary view, including requiredness, relevance-scoped duplicate-index findings, and cause-free default suppression; parallel field-pair validation keeps physical key membership separate from its declared frame traversal
- `owner`: [`ValidationRule.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationRule.lean), [`ValidationCondition/Iteration.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ValidationCondition/Iteration.lean), [`ParallelPresenceRule.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ParallelPresenceRule.lean), [partial-preliminary projection law](../../A12Kernel/Proofs/CheckedIndexPreliminary.lean)
- `assurance`: E/P closed for admitted loops, ordinary field leaves, and unfiltered Number aggregates, including three-level projection, empty-as-zero full/partial execution, no-outer control, nonmaterialization, row-exact admission, relevance-scoped duplicate suppression, requiredness, and a zero-bound once plan admitted by another concrete error row; upstream L locked at a12-dmkits [source registry](../SOURCES.md) for the row-domain and iteration-bound gate splits and [source registry](../SOURCES.md) for duplicate-partner relevance; selected C/X
- `parallel-pairs`: [`ParallelFieldPair.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ParallelFieldPair.lean) owns one certified two-group layout and key/frame traversal, shared by the existing positive-presence API and [`ParallelFieldRule.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ParallelFieldRule.lean). Fields retain their own declarations across kind-neutral presence, paired field-fill and shared Number expressions, whose atoms are a Number field, a temporal component or an aggregate over a field list, each read through the ordinary validation fold; a missing joined side is assumed to make the atom silently unavailable (`external evidence pending`). A distinct count against a literal stays refused, and the aggregate's mixed-reference iteration guard is assumed to be the ordinary entity-list one (`external evidence pending`). Both operand declarations, exact numeric scale admission, signed empty-to-zero polarity, invalid-index UNKNOWN and unknown error coordinates remain explicit.
- `parallel-cache-query`: Automatic full/partial retries use the [shared query context](validation-messages-and-custom.md#cap-structural-message-references), retaining whole-population hash effects without requiring unrelated associations to settle. [Query-isolation controls](../sources/message-cache-probes.md#src-message-cache-query-isolation) close the duplicate error-pointer refusal beside an unrelated host-sensitive collision. Explicit supplied caches keep priority and an unsettled requested coordinate retains `unresolvedDuplicatePointer`.
- `parallel-partial`: [Partial parallel evaluation](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/PartialParallelFieldRule.lean) and the reader's `runPartialParallelRule` reuse the certified pair, condition tree and partial generated-preliminary/cache views. Relevant index addresses select keys; compressed error-field selectors gate ordinary outer dispatch; exact matched reads and wildcard missing-side reads preserve silent unavailability separately from formal causes. Complete descendant coverage can establish a missing group's absence.
- `parallel-domain`: One-sided full/partial joins reuse [the validation iteration domain](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/RuleIterationDomain.lean), including uninstantiated slots in small declared spaces and occupied-prefix pruning in larger ones. Frame coordinates remain available even when only the unframed side supplies a key. Common outer joins retain their independent namespaces.
- `parallel-partial-assurance`: [Reader locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/PartialParallelRuleLocks.lean) cover six comparisons, seven fill operators, field/group presence, dispatch unions, wildcard-versus-concrete availability, Number aliases, duplicate cache population and frame bounds 3/6. [Generic laws](../../A12Kernel/Proofs/ParallelPresenceRule.lean) retain error-gate exclusion and cause-free unavailable presence. The [56-row capture](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-partial-parallel) supports 1,148 actual-model partial rule-row comparisons with matching message multisets; this is calibration, not a new broad cell or consumer qualification.
- `parent-partial-controls`: [Reader locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/PartialParallelRuleLocks.lean) distinguish matching and swapped keys in two parents, concrete outer selection, unknown-pointer multiplicity and mixed payload selectors. Compressed dispatch retains the original per-parent leaf set; these are internal compositions of the retained partial/outer matrices.
- `parallel-read-admission`: The reader runs the shared authored-reference walk against the error target before constructing the pair. An unbound ordinary frame reports `MVK_NO_WILDCARD` for an unrelated fixed target; the joined target is admitted. [Peer provenance](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-parallel-group-outer) owns the measured pair; a deeper fixed target is an internal separator.
- `parallel-number-keys`: The [checked join](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/CheckedIndexColumn.lean) admits matching Number kinds with dot-decimal storage, preserving a rendered iteration token beside each numeric lookup key. [Full-rule locks](../../A12Kernel/Conformance/ParallelNumberIndex.lean) and the existing reader locks cover repeated matched visits, lexical order, exact decimals, both missing sides and duplicate/invalid exclusion. The computation control matches by numeric identity and executes once per target. Existing column admission and missing-side laws apply.
- `parallel-number-evidence`: The [seven-document capture](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-parallel-number-index-tokens) calibrates positive/negative full-validation rules with equal maximum scales and differing minimum scales. Different maximum scales remain refused by the exact-kind certificate; other separators and wider nested numeric frames are not newly calibrated; the later partial matrix below has its own scope. This adds no broad cell or unchanged-population gain.
- `parallel-guards`: The existing condition-tree guard algebra checks the parallel level and each extra ordinary frame level independently. Conjunction needs a referenced guard; every disjunction branch must guard. A field-fill predicate that can fire from the unframed side cannot itself guard the frame. [Native locks](../../A12Kernel/Conformance/ParallelPresenceRule.lean) separate both frame orientations; the [registered certificate law](../../A12Kernel/Proofs/ParallelPresenceRule.lean) exposes typed leaves, both pair references and both guard obligations.
- `parallel-reader`: [`ParallelValidation.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/ParallelValidation.lean) lowers one indexed pair with independent payload declarations, Number arithmetic comparisons, plain GroupFilled/GroupNotFilled and And/Or. Group-only sides use topology anchors without inventing authored references. Declaring groups resolve paths; in-capacity common outer rows dispatch independent joins.
- `parallel-instance-leaves`: A leaf with no pair form is lowered by the ordinary reader against the [instance view](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ParallelInstanceView.lean), which makes the joined groups fixed, and evaluates as a model-free flat leaf over the joined row; an unmatched partner reads empty. [Instance-view locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/ParallelInstanceViewLocks.lean) replay text, cross-join Date, membership and its negation, String sides, partner text and `Length` over matched, key-only/unmatched and malformed partners; the [checkpoint](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-parallel-instance-leaves) owns 19 static and 48 runtime rule rows, all replayed.
- `parallel-ordinary-leaves`: A leaf the instance view has no flat form for is lowered against the model, the error side's group bound for a keyed presence when the declaring group refuses it, and becomes an [ordinary parallel leaf](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ParallelFieldRule.lean) when one [binding](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/ParallelFieldRule.lean) fixes its every iterating read: the common outer row, else the error side's joined row, else the partner's. Aggregates over stars or filters, literal keys into a third or the partner group, a key field of either side, a pattern over a side's field and a starred uniqueness test are admitted; reads below a joined group or in a repeatable frame enclosing one, `RepetitionNotUnique`, current-repetition and custom leaves are refused.
- `parallel-ordinary-evaluation`: Full and partial evaluation of an ordinary parallel leaf reuse the ordinary addressed evaluator at its binding; an unmatched side binds a free row of its group, read as a row holding only its key. References reuse the ordinary projection there, a read on a bound side's row counting as a joined read with its index field. An ordinary leaf guards no iteration. A distinct count or uniqueness test over one field of each side is instead a pair leaf over the two joined values. An atom without a joined form inside a pair comparison, such as the starred `Sum` in a joined operation's generated rule, is an ordinary one-atom leaf the comparison reads at its binding.
- `parallel-ordinary-evidence`: The [rule-check projection](../../A12Kernel/Evidence/RuleCheckProjection.lean) replays admission rows `p00`–`p02`; the [row-rendering lock](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/MessageRowRenderingLocks.lean) and population replay cover `RPk`; the [joined-leaf locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/ParallelJoinedGuardLocks.lean) replay the [joined-carrier](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-joined-carrier-admission) rules and generated rules with their messages and references, and the [joined-limits locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/ParallelJoinedLimitsLocks.lean) the [partner-key, key-only and pair-count rows](../sources/model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-joined-carrier-limits).
- `parallel-expression-controls`: [Full/partial reader locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/PartialParallelRuleLocks.lean) cover four distinct payloads, a foreign-root scalar, malformed/excluded payloads, two-parent selection and a second-payload error target. Shared numeric evaluation preserves fillability and unavailability. Model-owned field certificates reject forged declarations; ordinary and parallel guards retain literal-zero and mixed-wrapper distinctions.
