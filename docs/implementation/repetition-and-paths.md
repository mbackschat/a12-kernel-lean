- `difference-family-separation`: the three differ only in their pair gate and in which half of the stored temporal value they read, and one fixture separates all of them: the complete instant is admitted at the day and sub-day units and refused at the completed-period one, the date-only declaration is the mirror, and the day answer and the completed-period answer over the same two cells disagree, so they cannot both come from the same half. The day family certifies its model-zone profile against the model's own declared zone, which is why it needs no evaluation world. A Base-Year endpoint reads nothing and is admitted only at the year the model declares; `Now` is refused in every family, because this carrier's row read carries no evaluation world, and the sub-day family refuses `BaseYear` too.
- `direct-field-list-aggregate`: the direct field-list form of `Sum`, `MinValue`, `MaxValue` and `NumberOfDifferentValues` is **modelled**, and it needed no new scope rule: each member answers the same shared predicate a single field answers, with the list's own arity and duplicate gates beside it. The Kernel decides all four scope rows the same way — a nonrepeatable field outside the target's row is admitted, an unstarred read of a disjoint repeatable group is refused as a missing wildcard, a row-local field is admitted, and the target's own field is refused as a calculated-field reference ([checkpoint](../sources/group-and-iteration-probes.md#src-repetition-aggregate-operand-scope)) — which is exactly what `repetitionBoundBy` computes, because an empty repeatable scope is bound by any scope. The row-bound law therefore reaches through this arm instead of acquiring an exception.
- `measured-admitted-and-unmodelled`: the **starred** aggregate forms and the starred `NumberOfFilledFields` are measured admitted beside a repetition operand ([checkpoint](../sources/group-and-iteration-probes.md#src-repetition-numeric-source-kinds)) and stay unmodelled, because a star reads a whole declared extent that this carrier's row-scoped read cannot enumerate, and because a starred operand answers **no** scope rule at all — a star over a disjoint repeatable group and over a second root are both admitted. That freedom is the star's, not the aggregate family's, which is why the direct list above keeps the ordinary rule. `NumberOfFilledGroups` naming the target's own repeatable group is separately refused by the Kernel's calculated-field gate whatever the operand order.
- `remains`: Those, computation alternatives and scheduling, generated validation, parser/protocol exposure, and independent consumer qualification remain outside this capsule. [SG19](../gaps/paths-and-groups.md#gap-sg19) owns wider operand/binding work; [SG4](../gaps/keystone.md#gap-sg4) owns computation composition.
