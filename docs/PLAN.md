All three partial computed-Date target modes project their exact class while FULL remains admitted, both condition-pattern operators project their two pattern-source stages plus every represented non-String left kind to the exact measured classes, and raw-String `Length` enforces its exact lexical/range boundary while separating the two measured refusal classes. [`SG9`](SEMANTICS-GAPS.md#sg9--paths-indices-and-static-legality-completion) owns the broad axis; [`SG13`](SEMANTICS-GAPS.md#sg13--group-list-and-group-count-completion) owns the remaining filled-group runtime breadth.
