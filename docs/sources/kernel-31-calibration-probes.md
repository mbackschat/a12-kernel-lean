# Kernel 31.1.1 calibration checkpoints

Measurements this repository takes on kernel 31.1.1 through the [measurement checkout](cross-layer-routes.md#src-measurement-checkout-route), one checkpoint per capture, in the order the [upgrade playbook's first calibration](../KERNEL-UPGRADE-PLAYBOOK.md#5-first-calibration-on-the-new-kernel) runs them. Readings taken on kernel 30.8.1 stay in their family shards.

#### On kernel 31.1.1 partial validation skips a filtered `Sum`, count and value list, a `$` read inside a filter references no leaf, `CurrentRepetition` is refused at a skipped outer level's inner level, and a `SumOfTerms` computation's dividing row adds nothing, measured 2026-11-04

- `producer`: a12-dmkits `e62c2059d78f896a0b6304891c76c113966cd069`, the [measurement checkout](cross-layer-routes.md#src-measurement-checkout-route) clean before and after; dmtool `0.14.0`, Kernel `31.1.1` built and runtime. One `batch … --observations` of 17 rule checks and one `:adapter:kernelProbe` request observing `validateFull`, `validatePart` and `compute`, `source.state: CLEAN` in both artifacts and `enginesAgree: true` on all six runtime rows. This is the first measurement on kernel 31.1.1 in this repository.
- `models`: authored fresh with the structured verbs, since the retained 30.8.1 models carry a model format the 31.1.1 kernel refuses (`RK_MODEL_VERSION_INCOMPATIBLE`). `Claims31_DM` holds a claim list of four with scale-0 Numbers, six rules and the computation `Total := RoundDown(SumOfTerms(Claims*, [Claims/Amount] / [Claims/Reserve]), 0)`; the rounding is needed because the kernel refuses the division's unknown scale at the target (`MVK_INVALID_COMPARE_DEC_PLACES`). `IterGuard31_DM` reduces the [iteration-guard model](../../evidence/kernel-30.8.1/captures/iteration-guard-2026-10-22/) to `Lines` (50, keyed) and `Picks` (10, keyed). `PolicyKeyed_DM` and `PolicyPlain_DM` hold `Claims` with `Parts` (3), `Claims` repeating twice and keyed in the first and three times in the second.
- `computation`: over rows 10/2 and 6/0, and over 10/2 and 6 with no Reserve, `Total` is 5; over 10/2 and 6/3 it is 7; over the single row 6/0 it is 0. No outcome is cleared or errored.
- `partial`: over every field and the claim group, full validation fires every filtered rule and its unfiltered twin where its condition holds, and partial validation fires only the twins `PS0`, `PC0` and `PV0`: the filtered `Sum` `PS`, `NumberOfFilledFields` `PC` and `NoFieldValueIncludedInValueList` `PV` are skipped. Full validation types `PC0` VALUE and the others OMISSION.
- `static`: `f-and` and `f-or`, a `Sum` whose filter reads `$Picks/Ref` beside a negative and a positive `Picks` read, draw `MVK_NEG_CONDITION_IN_ITERATION`, and `f-list`, a filtered value list reading `$Picks/Ref` in its filter, is admitted; the controls `NoFieldValueIncludedInValueList(Picks/Ref In "X")` is refused and a `FieldFilled(Picks/Ref)`-guarded unfiltered list over `Lines` is admitted. On the keyed policy `FieldNotFilled(Claims/Amount)` is admitted, so the guard skips `Claims`, while `FieldNotFilled(Claims/Parts/Qty)` and `[Claims/Parts/Qty] > CurrentRepetition(Claims)` are refused and the comparison's `FieldFilled` guard admits it. The plain policy refuses all three bare rows.
