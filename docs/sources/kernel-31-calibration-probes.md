# Kernel 31.1.1 calibration checkpoints

Measurements this repository takes on kernel 31.1.1 through the [measurement checkout](cross-layer-routes.md#src-measurement-checkout-route), one checkpoint per capture, in the order the [upgrade playbook's first calibration](../KERNEL-UPGRADE-PLAYBOOK.md#5-first-calibration-on-the-new-kernel) runs them. Readings taken on kernel 30.8.1 stay in their family shards.

<a id="src-first-kernel-31-capture"></a>
#### On kernel 31.1.1 partial validation skips a filtered `Sum`, count and value list, a `$` read inside a filter references no leaf, `CurrentRepetition` is refused at a skipped outer level's inner level, and a `SumOfTerms` computation's dividing row adds nothing, measured 2026-11-04

- `producer`: a12-dmkits `e62c2059d78f896a0b6304891c76c113966cd069`, the [measurement checkout](cross-layer-routes.md#src-measurement-checkout-route) clean before and after; dmtool `0.14.0`, Kernel `31.1.1` built and runtime. One `batch … --observations` of 17 rule checks and one `:adapter:kernelProbe` request observing `validateFull`, `validatePart` and `compute`, `source.state: CLEAN` in both artifacts and `enginesAgree: true` on all six runtime rows. This is the first measurement on kernel 31.1.1 in this repository.
- `models`: authored fresh with the structured verbs, since the retained 30.8.1 models carry a model format the 31.1.1 kernel refuses (`RK_MODEL_VERSION_INCOMPATIBLE`). `Claims31_DM` holds a claim list of four with scale-0 Numbers, six rules and the computation `Total := RoundDown(SumOfTerms(Claims*, [Claims/Amount] / [Claims/Reserve]), 0)`; the rounding is needed because the kernel refuses the division's unknown scale at the target (`MVK_INVALID_COMPARE_DEC_PLACES`). `IterGuard31_DM` reduces the [iteration-guard model](../../evidence/kernel-30.8.1/captures/iteration-guard-2026-10-22/) to `Lines` (50, keyed) and `Picks` (10, keyed). `PolicyKeyed_DM` and `PolicyPlain_DM` hold `Claims` with `Parts` (3), `Claims` repeating twice and keyed in the first and three times in the second.
- `computation`: over rows 10/2 and 6/0, and over 10/2 and 6 with no Reserve, `Total` is 5; over 10/2 and 6/3 it is 7; over the single row 6/0 it is 0. No outcome is cleared or errored.
- `partial`: over every field and the claim group, full validation fires every filtered rule and its unfiltered twin where its condition holds, and partial validation fires only the twins `PS0`, `PC0` and `PV0`: the filtered `Sum` `PS`, `NumberOfFilledFields` `PC` and `NoFieldValueIncludedInValueList` `PV` are skipped. Full validation types `PC0` VALUE and the others OMISSION.
- `static`: `f-and` and `f-or`, a `Sum` whose filter reads `$Picks/Ref` beside a negative and a positive `Picks` read, draw `MVK_NEG_CONDITION_IN_ITERATION`, and `f-list`, a filtered value list reading `$Picks/Ref` in its filter, is admitted; the controls `NoFieldValueIncludedInValueList(Picks/Ref In "X")` is refused and a `FieldFilled(Picks/Ref)`-guarded unfiltered list over `Lines` is admitted. On the keyed policy `FieldNotFilled(Claims/Amount)` is admitted, so the guard skips `Claims`, while `FieldNotFilled(Claims/Parts/Qty)` and `[Claims/Parts/Qty] > CurrentRepetition(Claims)` are refused and the comparison's `FieldFilled` guard admits it. The plain policy refuses all three bare rows.
- `local`: [capture locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/FirstKernel31CaptureLocks.lean) hold every runtime row through the reader on the same model and the capture's document text; the [iteration-guard locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/IterationGuardLocks.lean) and [31.1.1 guard locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/IterationGuard31Locks.lean) hold the static rows, the `f-` rows on the full order model the reduced one was cut from. Lean agreed with every row before the change; the locks moved those rows from derived to measured.
- `limits`: one repeatable level, `en_US`, String filters only, one keyed outer group of two. The other partial filter carriers, a divisor other than a row field and a nested `SumOfTerms` entity are not measured.
- `bytes`: [Capture](../../evidence/kernel-31.1.1/captures/first-calibration-2026-11-04/) retains the four models, the request and artifact, and the rule-check operations and observations; rule messages are the rule codes. [Manifest](../../evidence/kernel-31.1.1/captures/first-calibration-2026-11-04/MANIFEST.sha256) SHA-256 `b7f04dde0c2682f84435e687c451d3aba6e1dda01cc246345489c3fceb4c9136`.
- `sync`: spec/07 §3.10, the spec/07 guard table and reference set, and spec/10 §5.2 move these rows from unmeasured to measured; Lean already gave every value, so no account changes and no ledger entry is needed.

#### Every retained 30.8.1 request and rule-check, declaration and model-check batch re-run unchanged on kernel 31.1.1 agrees with Lean after five corrections, and the population replay finds no divergence, measured 2026-11-04

- `producer`: a12-dmkits `e62c2059d78f896a0b6304891c76c113966cd069`, the [measurement checkout](cross-layer-routes.md#src-measurement-checkout-route) clean before and after every run; dmtool `0.14.0`, Kernel `31.1.1` built and runtime. Runtime requests ran through `:adapter:kernelProbe`'s JVM command line read once from Gradle and launched directly, four in parallel; a repeat run of `stamp-construction-2026-10-04/berlin` wrote byte-identical bytes. Rule checks ran through `dmtool batch … --observations` from each batch's own directory, dry-run and `apply` batches through the envelope's codes, self-contained message rows through generated `rule check` operations, and the patch-built declaration and model-check captures through `model check` on models rebuilt from their retained rows with their number tokens kept exact.
- `inputs`: the frozen requests, models, documents, operations and rows under `evidence/kernel-30.8.1/captures/`, each model restamped from DM 28.4.0 to 29.4.0 as the migrator does ([record](inbound-handbacks.md#src-inbound-2026-11-03c)) and nothing else changed; the [re-capture script](../../scripts/recapture-kernel-evidence.py) owns the runtime and registered rule-check routes.
- `runtime`: 606 of 611 requests wrote an artifact. Four `model-frame-check-route-2026-10-29` requests and `empty-enumeration-token-2026-10-14` wrote none because the Kernel builds or admits no model, as the frozen capture retained none for the first four. The [population replay](../TESTING.md#the-population-replay) over all 612 requests matched 241452 instances with no tier-1 divergence; 75 are tier 2 (the message-cache host order and seven host-preparation captures) and 28 absent (rows the Kernel faulted on or its engines disagree on), the same causes the [replay scope](../EVIDENCE.md#population-replay-scope) records. The frozen population's 1604 divergences were all version changes.
- `static`: all 255 registered rule-check batches, now 6196 compared rows, the 355 [declaration rows](model-structure-and-declaration-probes.md#src-field-declaration-notifications-k17) and the ten Number formal artifacts agree through the reader. Every row deferred to a 31.1.1 change now agrees, so those deferrals and the code rename map are deleted. Five registry entries that compared nothing, because they named their observations as their requests, now name their operations. Three rows stay deferred: `empty-enumeration-token` `s03`, `s06` and `s07`, which carry the classes of the model's own rules.
- `corrections`: `$RuleGroup.Vordruckzeile$` draws `INVALID_FIELD_IN_PARAMETER` (`I01`, `P01`), where 30.8.1 faulted internally; `$RuleGroup.Vordruckname$` draws `MVK_UNEXPECTED_TOKEN` (`P00`), where Lean had derived `MVK_INCOMPLETE_INPUT`; a declared empty Enumeration token is no literal (`s01`, `s02`, `s05`), which a12-dmkits had already corrected at `fe4d26e14` and the M4–M6 adoption missed; a bodiless DateFragment, an absent `formatOfFragment`, leaves no model (`tb-frag-nobody`). The fifth was a defect of the first run: ten self-contained message files had been copied rather than measured, and their re-capture moves `P2` to `INVALID_ENTITY`, Lean's value.
- `unreplayed`: the model-consistency, sort-list, Enumeration-category, declaration-admission, empty rule-group and String-ingestion captures, whose locks transcribe their rows, were re-run and diffed against their frozen observations. Every changed row is a delta Lean already states for 31.1.1: the deleted value-limit rule (peer KF582), the converter's blank-name and empty-reference sentences, the index name that now builds, and an absent `errorCode` that leaves no model.
- `local`: the [rule-check](../../A12Kernel/Evidence/RuleCheckProjection.lean), [declaration](../../A12Kernel/Evidence/DeclarationNotificationProjection.lean) and [Number formal](../../A12Kernel/Evidence/NumberFormalProjection.lean) projections read the frozen inputs and the 31.1.1 observations, and `lake test` runs them.

The re-capture measures only what the frozen inputs ask, so a construct new in 31.1.1, such as `SumOfTerms`, is measured only by the [first capture](#src-first-kernel-31-capture) and a12-dmkits' rows.
- `bytes`: [`evidence/kernel-31.1.1/captures/`](../../evidence/kernel-31.1.1/captures/) retains the rule-check, declaration and Number formal observations `lake test` reads. The runtime artifacts stay in the ignored `evidence/kernel-31.1.1/local/`; [`RECAPTURE.sha256`](../../evidence/kernel-31.1.1/RECAPTURE.sha256), SHA-256 `7059cf19d9775ab67ed399f9b17868147a11366eee8c6196d15297ecbca2bada`, holds the digest of each of the 606.
