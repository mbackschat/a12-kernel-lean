- `question`: the [starred group-count checkpoint](#src-starred-group-count-computation) reported `OMISSION_ERROR` on every row, including ones whose computed value was three or four against a stored seed, where the [fixed-operand capture](#src-repeatable-descendant-group-count) had measured the ordinary split. Two captures over two models confound the operand list with the model, so the first job was a single-run witness and the second was finding the boundary of whatever it showed.
- `witness`: one model, one document, one run, four Number computations at the same root group, every target seeded to mismatch. `NumberOfFilledGroups(Flat, Other)`, carrying no wildcard, types **`VALUE_ERROR`**. `NumberOfFilledGroups(Rows*)`, `NumberOfFilledGroups(Flat, Rows*)`, and `Sum(Rows*/RowValue)` all type **`OMISSION_ERROR`**, with `FlatValue`, `OtherValue`, and both `RowValue`s filled. - `claim`: **the type follows whether a legal move can still falsify the fired condition, and nothing about starredness as such.** `/Probe/Rows` is declared `max 5`. At **five** instantiated rows with every cell filled, all four targets type `VALUE_ERROR` — the starred ones included. At **four** rows with every cell filled, the three starred targets type `OMISSION_ERROR` while the wildcard-free one stays `VALUE_ERROR`. One row of remaining capacity is the entire difference.
- `separator`: at five rows with every row **empty**, one document separates two growth channels over the same starred group. `NumberOfFilledGroups(Rows*)` and the mixed list type `VALUE_ERROR`, because capacity is exhausted and a count grows only by rows. `Sum(Rows*/RowValue)` types `OMISSION_ERROR` in that same document, because a row's empty cell can still be filled. So headroom is read per operator against its own quantity, not once per operand.
- `not the discriminator`: the witness **corroborates** the headroom rule and cannot establish it. A wildcard-free operand at `VALUE` beside a starred one at `OMISSION` is exactly what the refuted operand-form account predicts, so every below-capacity row is consistent with both readings. Only the at-capacity empty-row document chooses between them, because it holds operand form constant and the two starred targets still differ. a12-dmkits corrected this project's stronger label for its own witness, against its own contribution rather than for it, and completed the witness on its model at `8b7f602a3`.
- `not the operator`: what the below-capacity rows *do* rule out is a group-count-specific account. `Sum(Rows*/RowValue)` types `OMISSION` there alongside the two counts, and an account naming the operator predicts `VALUE` for it. That is a narrower conclusion than the one first drawn, and it survives.
- `correction`: this refutes the reading the witness alone invited, and which an earlier draft of this record and its outbound entry both stated — *a starred operand types `OMISSION` even when every concrete referenced cell is filled*. It is false at capacity. Every row of the witness, and a12-dmkits' independent two-row measurement of the same split, were taken **below** capacity, where the overreach is invisible; the two measurements are sound and the sentence drawn from them was not. It was caught before the outbound entry was committed, by measuring the discriminator instead of recording it as open.

Counts are grow-only, and `OMISSION` is typed exactly when a legal move can falsify the fired condition. - `question`: this project's `numberOfFilledGroups` made the count unknown on **any** erroneous operand, and `spec/02` said so in as many words. - `confirmed`: **the count agrees with `GroupFilled`, and the old clause was wrong.** `Unavail_DM` declares nonrepeatable `G1` (String `A`, Number `Num`) and `G2` (String `B`, Number `Num2`) beside a `max 3` repeatable `Rows`, with rules `NumberOfFilledGroups(G1, G2) < 1/2/3`.
