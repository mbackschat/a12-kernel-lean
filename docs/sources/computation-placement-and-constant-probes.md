#### All three temporal difference operand gates read the declared format and never the declared kind, measured locally 2026-09-07

#### Composition is one-way: an aggregate nests inside a wrapper, but no expression nests inside an aggregate's operand list, measured locally 2026-09-05

- `revision`: launcher self-reported build a12-dmkits `226b2be175133dff45413b0a0f604ea219ced85b (dirty)`; `dmtool` 0.13.0, Kernel `30.8.1`. Seven `computation add --dry-run` children in one `batch`, every one `KERNEL_CONFIRMED`. Retained `raw-nesting.json` SHA-256 `a0240381e5ca47fe6e5cabe39e6309ac35b3d7f88e7c21a9f18b7750c3e77f0e`, model `Scope_DM.json` SHA-256 `d99c474a41dc914231338e09e1f4492257597933826bfc0f6964c585bf0dec90`.
- `question`: [SG5](../SEMANTICS-GAPS.md#sg5--numeric-authoring-and-target-completion) carried deeper nested calls and aggregate-valued operands as open rows outside the power carrier.
- `claim`: **composition runs one way.** `Abs` nests to **depth three** admitted; an aggregate is admitted **inside** a wrapper, `Abs(Sum(G*/Base))`, and as one operand of an extremum, `Min(Sum(G*/Base), [F])`. The reverse is refused: a wrapper inside an aggregate's operand list, `Sum(Abs([G/Base]))`, is `MVK_UNEXPECTED_TOKEN` naming the wrapper.
- `generalizes-an-earlier-one-off`: that refusal is the **same code on the same shape** as the [carrier checkpoint](group-and-iteration-probes.md#src-currentrepetition-operand-carriers)'s `Sum(CurrentRepetition(G))`, which was recorded there as a fact about that one operand. Two independent operand kinds — a repetition function and an arithmetic wrapper — refused identically is what turns it into a rule about the **position**: an aggregate's operand list takes field paths, not expressions of any kind.
- `controls`: a plain `[F] * 2` and a depth-one `Abs([F])` into the same target are admitted through the identical route, so the refusal is not the target's or the route's.
- `limit`: static admission only, one model, `en_US`. Depth three is what was measured and no bound was searched for, so nothing here says whether a deeper nest is admitted or where it would stop; only `Abs`, `Min`, and `Sum` are exercised as the composing operators.
- `sync`: none. It closes two of this project's own open rows and generalizes one of its own earlier records; neither estate's clause said an aggregate operand accepted expressions, so nothing is corrected. [`spec/04`](../../spec/04-numbers-and-decimals.md) carries the one-way rule and [`spec/07`](../../spec/07-repetition-and-iteration.md) drops its operand-specific phrasing in favour of it.

#### `^` restricts neither operand's shape; a non-literal exponent derives **unknown** scale, and that is what a computed target refuses, measured locally 2026-09-05

- `revision`: launcher self-reported build a12-dmkits `226b2be175133dff45413b0a0f604ea219ced85b (dirty)`; `dmtool` 0.13.0, Kernel `30.8.1`. Sixteen children across three `batch` runs — seven `computation add --dry-run` and nine `rule check` — every one `KERNEL_CONFIRMED`.
- `retained`: `raw-power-shapes.json` SHA-256 `6543afaee43ca4ed6f5353a4d3873c91a1a72e4e1dc136f14debbdc9a34baa9a`, `raw-power-condition.json` SHA-256 `933a2ff81e48ab7bd76648c3ff5e2480fc108579d8f3124d1cfee30532990de9`, `raw-power-equality.json` SHA-256 `c7e80eff94dd99315065f925bf790d2a5d63a79543eb39e04266581289a690b4`, model `Scope_DM.json` SHA-256 `d99c474a41dc914231338e09e1f4492257597933826bfc0f6964c585bf0dec90`.
- `question`: [SG5](../SEMANTICS-GAPS.md#sg5--numeric-authoring-and-target-completion) carried two named rows — aggregate-valued operands, and power beyond a bounded field-or-literal child. The computation arm alone answers them **wrongly**, which is why the condition arm was run.
- `computation-arm`: writing a scale-0 Number target, `[F] ^ 2`, `{ [F] + 1 } ^ 2`, and `Sum(G*/Base) ^ 2` are all **admitted** — so a grouped arithmetic expression and an aggregate are both legal bases. `[F] ^ [F]`, `[F] ^ { [F] + 1 }`, and `[F] ^ Sum(G*/Base)` are all refused `MVK_INVALID_COMPARE_DEC_PLACES`, whose text names scales rather than structure: *"the maximum number of fractional digits of the left-hand side (0) and of the right-hand side (?) are different"*.
- `claim`: **the operator restricts neither operand's shape, and the refusals belong to the target comparison.** On the **validation** arm every exponent shape is admitted under an ordering comparison — field, grouped expression, and aggregate alike — so nothing structural rejects a non-literal exponent. What a non-literal exponent does is derive an **unknown** scale, `?`, and unknown scale then meets the equality-scale gate the clause already owns.
- `separator`: a 2×2 in one model on the condition arm, holding the expression fixed and varying only the comparison and the exponent. Under `==`, a **literal** exponent is admitted and a **field** exponent is refused `MVK_INVALID_COMPARE_DEC_PLACES`; under `>`, the field exponent is admitted. A plain `==` between two fields is the fourth cell, admitted. So the code follows the comparison operator and the derived scale, not the power — and the computation arm refuses because its generated comparison is an equality against a target of known scale.
- `what-is-new`: [`spec/04`](../../spec/04-numbers-and-decimals.md) gives derived scale for `+`/`−` (max), `×` (sum), and `÷` (unknown) and omits `^` entirely; it also already states the equality-scale gate and its `@SuppressWarning` waiver. The missing rule is the power's own: **unknown unless the exponent is a literal**. Everything else here follows from clauses already written, which is why the entry claims only that one addition.
- `the-waiver-closes-it`: **the unknown scale is waivable, and the waiver is what shows the shape is legal rather than tolerated.** A computation carrying `errorCodesToSuppress: ["MVK_INVALID_COMPARE_DEC_PLACES"]` admits `[F] ^ [F]` and `[F] ^ Sum(G*/Base)` into the same scale-0 target that refuses them without it — an exact minimal pair, one key apart. The same key admits `[F] / 2`, so one waiver serves both unknown-scale operators rather than power having a rule of its own. Retained `raw-power-suppression.json` SHA-256 `e70b671928a7fef9ce0a89cb58169ea51559d3a6eb035a37bbc13b720494c8de`.
- `propagates-through-an-extremum`: the derived scale **composes**. Inside `Min(…, [F])`, a power with a literal exponent is admitted, a **grouped** base with a literal exponent is admitted, and a field exponent is refused with the same `MVK_INVALID_COMPARE_DEC_PLACES` — waived by the same `errorCodesToSuppress`. So the extremum neither introduces nor absorbs the unknown scale; it carries it to the target comparison unchanged, which is what closes [SG5](../SEMANTICS-GAPS.md#sg5--numeric-authoring-and-target-completion)'s grouped-addressed-power row. Retained `raw-power-in-extremum.json` SHA-256 `198aa17bb85e3256c9d1f9c96ae30c82a9bbfde8ea3182d75edee012ef17d3e2`.
- `confirms-the-fragment`: [`AddressedNumberPower.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/AddressedNumberPower.lean) already required the result-scale suppression and carries a `scaleSuppressionRequired` error for its absence. These rows are the Kernel evidence for that requirement, which the [coverage owner](../implementation/numeric.md) had recorded as covering **non-suppressed** placements only.
