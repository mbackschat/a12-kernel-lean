# Rule-set meta-information source checkpoints

Checkpoints for the Kernel's rule-collection analysis services — derivations over a rule set rather than over a document. Document-level validation and computation routes stay in [`cross-layer-routes.md`](cross-layer-routes.md); [`../SOURCES.md`](../SOURCES.md) remains the anchor registry for both.

#### Mandatory-information derivation reads a **32-bit-narrowed** threshold, measured locally 2026-08-31

- `revision`: thirteen `:adapter:kernelProbe` requests at a12-dmkits `c2900fe7c91a55e0debd0124f847ee2de0f06186`, the sibling checkout clean before and after with `state: CLEAN` in every artifact. `dmtool` 0.13.0, Kernel `30.8.1` built and runtime. Model-level `mandatoryInformation` observation, no document and no rows — the capability a12-dmkits built for [`EXP-2026-08-30-01`](../A12-DMKITS-SPEC-SYNC-LEDGER.md#exp-2026-08-30-01).
- `question`: `FeldOperationUtil.getZahlKonstanteWert` returns `(int) Math.round(Double.parseDouble(value))` for a numeric constant and `-1` for "not a numeric constant", so a rounding **and** a 32-bit narrowing sit in front of one sentinel. The entry asked whether the derived output distinguishes an authored literal from absence.
- `shape-found`: the entry's own `>= z` shape derives **nothing at all**, `mandatoryRootGroups` included, so it cannot discriminate. The shape that reaches the arm is the error-condition spelling of the same requirement, `NumberOfFilledFields(A, B) < z` — which yields the root group and no field. The positive control is a different shape entirely: `FieldNotFilled(A)` derives `/S1_DM/A` into `mandatory` and `mandatoryForRootGroup` and `S1_DM` into `mandatoryRootGroups`, so an empty result is a reading rather than a broken route.
- `claim`: **the derived answer follows the narrowed value and the guard is `> 0`.** Over one two-field list, `< 1` and `< 3` derive the root group; `< 0` and `< -1` do not. Four adjacent literals around `2^32` reproduce that boundary exactly: `< 4294967295` (narrows to `-1`) and `< 4294967296` (to `0`) derive nothing, while `< 4294967297` (to `1`) and `< 4294967299` (to `3`) derive it. `< -0.6` rounds onto `-1` and derives nothing.
- `witness`: **`< 3` against `< 4294967295` is the isolating pair.** Both are trivially true over a two-field count, so satisfiability is held constant and cannot explain the split; the models are identical but for the literal. A reader takes `4294967295` as a *stronger* requirement, and the derivation drops the rule. `< 4294967297` against `< 4294967295` closes it from the other side: adjacent literals, opposite answers, following the narrowed value and nothing else.
- `not-reached`: **this is the `z > 0` guard, not the `z != -1` sentinel arm.** The entry's competing accounts were about conflating a genuine `-1` with absence, and at a `z > 0` site the two are rejected alike, so the conflation stays unreached and unmeasured. What is measured is the narrowing in front of it, which the entry's `narrowed surface` row had called observationally inert at these sites — inert for separating the sentinel from a genuine `-1`, but not inert at all: a legal Kernel-accepted rule silently leaves the derivation, or silently derives at a different threshold, because its literal exceeds `2^31 - 1`.
- `field-sets`: every count-shaped rule leaves `mandatory` and `mandatoryForRootGroup` **empty** while moving `mandatoryRootGroups`, so this route observes the root-group set only. The field-set half of the entry's prediction is untestable on this shape; `FieldNotFilled` is the shape that populates them.
- `limit`: one two-field nonrepeatable list, one operator, `en_US`, no repetition, no `Having`. Not measured: a repeatable list, the other five `getZahlKonstanteWert` call sites, whether a rule dropped here is also dropped by any other analysis service, and the sentinel-versus-genuine-`-1` conflation itself.
- `bytes`: [`mandatory-information-narrowing/`](../../evidence/kernel-30.8.1/captures/mandatory-information-narrowing/), ten models with their requests and artifacts. SHA-256 of the isolating pair's models `87b9e61ae169ef051a6281d89a4f61ddb1170249e2a241aec81d656e0b50d4fb` (`< 3`) and `d030f7d70631db76cfe64d99438fd213a48774c01e84e673283020bcd21f5948` (`< 4294967295`), and of their artifacts `ba63424d1cff1452eacaaee17eb1677f0dd244c99a1a679520b404591402afc7` and `2a98fde10a5069ecb78e006b5f5294dac4ec91f2188391e5b66a6b41ec94433f`. Bounded raw calibration captures with no typed consumer: they do not run under `lake test` and establish neither `L` nor `C` coverage.
- `sync`: **no `spec/` change.** [`spec/`](../../spec/) has no section for rule-set meta-information, and opening one is a scope decision rather than a clause edit; [SG14](../SEMANTICS-GAPS.md#sg14--mandatory-information-derivation) owns the obligation and now owns this measurement. So this checkpoint carries no outbound entry, and [`EXP-2026-08-30-01`](../A12-DMKITS-SPEC-SYNC-LEDGER.md#exp-2026-08-30-01) records the answer in place.
