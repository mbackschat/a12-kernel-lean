- `local-consequence`: none. No new conformance case: the branches are already represented, and a per-row fixture would exercise the same two functions the flat cases cover.
- `limit`: one repetition level, three rows, Number targets only, one join of arity two, `validateFull` plus `compute` on one document pair. Not measured: two repetition levels, a diamond whose branches sit at different levels, a join reading across rows, and a repeatable diamond under an over-limit row.
- `sync`: locally originated, and it **confirms** the flat account and the existing per-instance confinement rule on a new carrier rather than correcting either, so it opens no ledger entry and dispositions none.
