## Conclusion

The bounded Explain task is cheap and useful. Its static-versus-runtime distinction transports without renewed archaeology across DateTime shifts, nested Time construction, FullDate extraction, String-shaped direct/literal Enumeration, homogeneous and heterogeneous whole-call operation lists, and one generated-preliminary parent-local DateRange scan.
