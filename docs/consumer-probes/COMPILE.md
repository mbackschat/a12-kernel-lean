# Compile consumer lab

- `status`: green
- `contract`: a checked A12 artifact becomes an executable specialized plan or target program under a named refinement relation.
- `authority`: the shared checked condition-tree evaluator and its [three-valued control-flow capability](../IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md#cap-shared-condition-tree-control-flow), plus the [checked direct-field formal-input inventory](../IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md#cap-checked-direct-field-formal-input) and [generated computation validation](../IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md#cap-generated-computation-validation).
- `handover`: the logic/formal-error and computation-order clauses plus one finite admitted source condition or generated numeric table.

## First probe

Compile one bounded source-first conjunction over the finite `value`, `empty`, and `formal` observation domain into a standalone specialized decision function. Exhaustively compare every admitted input pair against the Lean-owned truth, poison, and read-order projection.

The finite domain makes exhaustive refinement meaningful for this exact plan. It does not prove a general compiler. Wrong accounts are ordinary Boolean collapse, eager right evaluation, formal-to-false conversion, and reordered reads.

## Generated-table probe

Compile one admitted two-alternative generated numeric table into a standalone specialized selector and static input query. The table has a common `FieldFilled` guard, distinct row-local `FieldFilled` and `FieldNotFilled` guards, and operations with distinct dependencies. Exhaustively compare the specialized selector with an independently structured generic evaluator over the 27 `empty`/`value`/`malformed` assignments, then reconcile every selection outcome and the complete target-excluding input projection with Lean.

This refinement covers common-before-local evaluation, first-match selection, reached poison, and static all-alternatives dependency projection for this table. It does not execute the numeric operations or establish a general compiler, scheduler, generated-validation runtime, protocol, or shipment. Wrong accounts are common-guard omission, local-before-common evaluation, later-alternative omission, Boolean complement collapse, poison-to-false conversion, runtime-selected dependencies, retained computed targets, and artifact traversal order.
