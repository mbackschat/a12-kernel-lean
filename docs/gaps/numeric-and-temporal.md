### SG5 — numeric authoring and target completion

- `state`: open
- `remaining`: **the derived-scale gate's suppression branch is unmodelled, and that is an over-refusal.** `MVK_INVALID_COMPARE_DEC_PLACES` is the Kernel's one suppressible code, so a condition carrying `@SuppressWarning(MVK_INVALID_COMPARE_DEC_PLACES)` is admitted where a carrier applying the gate unconditionally refuses.
