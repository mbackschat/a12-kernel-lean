# Numeric and temporal residuals

[K01–K22](../PLAN.md#remaining-conformance-checklist) selects the required closure work. The [routing table](../PLAN.md#remaining-gap-routing) maps every family and historical D exclusion to its current task or consumer phase. D labels below describe the former C scope, not a reason to skip a K obligation. The [gap index](../SEMANTICS-GAPS.md) defines residual status; implemented behavior stays in the linked capability owners. Task selection supersedes conditional reopening language for its named acceptance cases.

### SG5 — numeric authoring and target completion

- `state`: residual; D01/D06/D12/D18/D20 cover additional depth, diagnostics, ingress, legacy APIs and calibration.
- `baseline`: [Number semantics](../implementation/numeric.md), [authoring class projection](../implementation/computations.md#cap-rule-condition-class-projection) and [group admission](../IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md#cap-shared-entity-list-group-admission) own the maintained routes.
- `host-boundary`: JSON comments the Kernel's document mapper permits (peer [KF1](../../../a12-rulekit/docs/KERNEL-FINDINGS.md#kf1), source-read), compact scientific rendering of a native number beyond the represented scale bound into a String field, and the document `/id` metadata conversion are document-ingress profile items the [host-input contract](../IMPLEMENTER-GUIDE.md#host-input-contract) owns; quoted conversion and the scale profile are closed at [SG27's baseline](#gap-sg27). [Wire provenance](../sources/cross-layer-routes.md#src-number-wire-ingestion) and [the inline-route handback](../sources/inbound-handbacks.md#src-inbound-2026-10-09a) distinguish native-number ingestion from probe serialization.
- `calibration`: deeper correlation, additional addressed/group expression placements and target profiles beyond the current Number matrices. The [tolerance capture](../sources/computation-placement-and-constant-probes.md#src-tolerance-family-calibration) owns C18; no ordinary tolerance cell remains queued here.
- `calibration`: the message-key shape of a field carrying the `-1` fractional sentinel is read from source only; its codes, resolved pair and printed bounds are closed at the [bounds checkpoint](../sources/domain-ingestion-probes.md#src-number-declaration-bounds-k17).
- `representation-boundary`: a `-1` maximum's unknown static scale is measured on every reader carrier in the [unknown-scale batch](../sources/static-admission-and-class-probes.md#src-unknown-scale-and-root-index), and reader admission agrees. The certificates of the addressed row computations, `Having` trees, correlation well-formedness and the repeatable constant and fixed-group-count computations still compare declared Nat scales, so such a carrier built directly from a `-1`-maximum field checks it at its minimum; only the reader's summary gate refuses it first.
- `representation-boundary`: a bare-Rat flat condition cannot retain authored literal scale. The [reader preflight](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/LowerValidation.lean) and [scale locks](../../A12Kernel/Reference/Dm/LowerLocks.lean) enforce it before that carrier; this is not an ordinary-reader admission defect.
- `representation-boundary`: the DSL reader records an integer literal's fraction length as its authored scale, so `100` reads scale 0, while [spec/04 §1](../../spec/04-numbers-and-decimals.md) and the semantic scale summary strip it to −2; the conformance cases build the stripped literal directly. No witness is known whose admitted result differs between the two scales.
- `new-class-31.3`: kernel 31.3.1's `MVK_ERROR_INVALID_FIELDTYPE` is raised, by source, for a tolerance operator under `Exact` on a computed field that is no Number, but the generated comparison's tolerance gate answers first on every String, Date and Custom target [measured](../sources/kernel-31-calibration-probes.md#src-tolerance-band-target-kinds); no admitted witness is known on the 31.3.1 pin, and Lean names no carrier for it. Discriminator: a target kind whose tolerance comparison the gate admits.
- `representation-boundary`: a power result inside the scale range can still raise at a comparison's or a target's rescale; Lean's rational values carry no scale, so it compares such a value, impractically at that size. Discriminator: `small-in-range-just-in` of `PowA` and `PowB1` in the [power-range capture](../sources/kernel-31-calibration-probes.md#src-power-scale-range).
- `representation-boundary`: Lean reads a power's base in its shortest decimal form, while the host keeps the trailing zeros its arithmetic produced, so near the bound, within about twice the working precision in scale units, the two can disagree on a product's scale. No measured row lies in that band.
- `carrier-limit`: a rule's numeric comparison, flat or ordered, reports the raise under full and partial validation through the result evaluators. The verdict-only APIs, `Having` bodies, `SumOfTerms` terms, temporal shift amounts and the parallel and correlated carriers read a raising power as unavailable and stay silent, and so does a numeric comparison in a computation's precondition or condition, which has no channel for the raise. In a computation a `SumOfTerms` term reads the raise as the domain failure the sum skips, as a division by zero there is measured to be skipped, and so do the library-only addressed power and extremum leaves, whose single power raises only for a base of millions of digits. The Kernel's answer on all of these is unmeasured; for the term the discriminator is a `SumOfTerms` computation one of whose terms raises.
- `consumer-gated`: parser nesting ceilings beyond the measured [power/extremum shapes](../sources/computation-placement-and-constant-probes.md#src-power-derived-scale-and-operand-shapes). Further depth is D06, not a missing power mechanism.

### SG6 — temporal authoring, calendar, and target completion

- `state`: residual; D03 wider calendars/formats, D20/D21 additional carriers and D23 broader calibration.
- `baseline`: [Temporal capabilities](../implementation/temporal.md), [DateRange capabilities](../implementation/date-range.md) and [recursive Having operations](../implementation/having-filters.md#cap-having-temporal-operations) own represented forms and exact supported profiles.
- `host-boundary`: below-Gregorian-floor/nonpositive-era dates, additional model zones, formats outside the declaration denominator and imprecise stored DateFragment forms require an explicit calendar/input profile. Unsupported profile information must not become an invented value or Kernel formal error.
- `representation-boundary`: the flat temporal comparison literal surface is narrower than the shared scalar temporal reader. Keep Time/DateTime literal refusals attached to that API rather than claiming those constructs are absent from maintained validation.
- `calibration`: DateFromDateTime topology, no-value cause, later-validation and application compositions beyond the [extractor owner](../implementation/temporal.md#cap-date-from-datetime).
- `calibration`: extractor and ValueAsDate wrong-kind classes for declared formats beyond the [K22 S2 checkpoint](../sources/group-count-topology.md#src-k22-static-s2)'s model, which measures every extractor and ValueAsDate over six kinds in a rule and a computation (`tl`).
- `calibration`: partial shift amounts beyond the [count-amount twin checkpoint](../sources/group-count-topology.md#src-k22-runtime-r11): DateTime distinct counts, Date and Enumeration value counts, fixed or mixed group-count amounts and a filtered temporal distinct-count amount in a row-bound rule, each taking its comparison twin's measured gate.
- `host-boundary`: an unconvertible String component of a temporal extraction left of a malformed operand in Number arithmetic, which Lean ends as a reached value failure by the unresolvable label's measured order ([operand-channel checkpoint](../sources/group-count-topology.md#src-k22-operands-channel)). No DM-JSON witness is known as of the measurement pin: a digit-pattern component always converts, and the alternative admitting other text has no DM-JSON carrier by a [source reading](../sources/static-admission-and-class-probes.md#src-extensible-enumeration-has-no-dm-json-carrier), not a measurement (spec/05). A host supplying that alternative must calibrate the order.
- `calibration`: additional configured computation profiles, yearless scalar-plus-star overlaps and non-stored/filtered repeatable overlap compositions beyond the [shared overlap owner](../implementation/date-range.md#cap-resolved-date-range-overlap-truth-admission-and-scans).
- `calibration`: endpoint-component readers beyond the [shared-runtime matrix](../sources/date-range-construction.md#src-date-range-shared-runtime) and direct first-filled lists beyond the measured arity.
- `calibration`: temporal distinct-count arithmetic/scheduling compositions, nested-group computation extents and component-omitting computation profiles beyond the [typed distinct-count owner](../implementation/strings-and-enumerations.md#cap-temporal-distinct-count).
- `calibration`: `DifferenceInDays` reading an exact instant a chained shift keeps inside Berlin's ambiguous hour; the [overlap-instant checkpoint](../sources/repeatable-temporal-computation-probes.md#src-overlap-instant-in-computation) measures a computation's comparison and sub-day difference of such an instant, and `DifferenceInDays` only on rows that do not separate it from a re-read rendering.
- `representation-boundary`: the flat validation entry points `resolveNumericValidationAtom`, `evalSelectedResult` and `evalSelected` have no failure channel and read a date constant without an instant (a Julian leap day under Europe/Berlin, [spec/05 §1](../../spec/05-dates-and-time.md#1-constant-format-and-the-stringdate-literal-ambiguity-)) as formally malformed, where the addressed route every model rule runs fails the rule; `differenceConstantFails` in [`NumericValidation/Evaluation.lean`](../../A12Kernel/Elaboration/NumericValidation/Evaluation.lean) owns the split.
- `representation-boundary`: Lean refuses two temporal computations the Kernel refuses but names no class for them: a shift whose operand is its own computed target, which draws `MVK_ERROR_REFERENCE_TO_CALCULATED_FIELD` (`temporal-target-gate-reads-format-not-kind` rows 403 and 405), and an operation written as the bare word `Time`, which draws `MVK_EXPECTED_TOKEN_NOT_FOUND` (rows 509–512); the [rule-check projection](../../A12Kernel/Evidence/RuleCheckProjection.lean) defers the six rows ([verdict-file pass](../sources/kernel-31-calibration-probes.md#src-kernel-31-recapture-verdicts)).
- `calibration`: a pre-cutover `ValueAsDate` shift landing is derived for kernel 31.1.1 rather than measured, and which occurrence an autumn-fold `DateTime(date, time)` construction chooses is unmeasured on 31.1.1.
- `reopen-when`: a selected consumer needs one named profile or a current admitted case contradicts its oracle. No additional zone, depth or format campaign is implied.
- `route-state`: discovery-required

### SG27 — Number presentation information and the remaining formal key families

- `state`: residual; the presentation-information host profile is D12.
- `baseline`: [Stored Number formal read](../implementation/numeric.md#cap-stored-number-formal-read), [formal cascade](../implementation/numeric.md#cap-number-formal-cascade) and [message keys](../implementation/numeric.md#cap-runtime-formal-message-keys) own quoted conversion, kept leading-zero text, every cascade arm and the Number key selection, calibrated by the [K16 capture](../sources/domain-ingestion-probes.md#src-number-ingress-k16).
- `host-boundary`: caller-supplied presentation information — a grouping separator checked and removed before the cascade, a non-dot decimal separator and optional decimal places — and the display-to-internal conversion service. The measured document route supplies no optional decimal places; a grouping-separator route is unmeasured. The reader refuses a decimal scale above 100,000 in magnitude rather than reproducing the host fault.
- `calibration`: a computed Number's rendering under a declared `,` decimal separator; stored reads and literals under `,` are measured by the [declared-comma checkpoint](../sources/domain-ingestion-probes.md#src-declared-comma-separator).
- `reopen-when`: a named ingress or rendering consumer needs presentation information or another key family. Do not reopen the completed C11 lexical-length matrix.
- `route-state`: discovery-required
- `calibration-31.3`: the fifteen-digit cap of the stored read text ([31.3.1 Letter J](../sources/inbound-handbacks.md#src-inbound-2026-11-07) §4) is measured on its ledger pad, a seventeen-digit rate and the half-up pair; unmeasured and kept as the source-read account: the floor at zero for a value of sixteen or more integer digits, a value below one counting zero or negative integer digits so that its cap passes fifteen, and a String-valued placement, which stays verbatim.
