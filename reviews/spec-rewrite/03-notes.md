Side taken: spec/02 A.4.1 (recursive), already decided there by spec/02's decision 4; nested non-repeatable subgroups kept unmeasured as spec/02 says.
3. All-empty concatenation as a comparison operand (ch03-7). Old 03:40: "a String comparison with an empty operand on *either* side … is **not evaluated**". Old 03:56: "concatenates as `""`, so the enclosing comparison **is** evaluated". Side taken: the comparison is decided by the concatenation's result; an all-empty concatenation (`""`) is not evaluated. Lean: `CheckedStringExpressionComparison.operand` and `StringExpr.concatOperands` (`A12Kernel/Elaboration/StringExpression.lean`, `A12Kernel/Semantics/StringComputation.lean`).
