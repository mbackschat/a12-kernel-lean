# Structural audit of `spec/`, 2026-10-26

This report covers all `spec/` chapters 01–12 and `SEMANTICS-MAP.md` at repository revision `d780801f` and later. Method: one read-only opus auditor per chapter group applied a fixed class scheme (C1 contradiction or override, C2 enumeration without rule, C3 undecided case, C4 content delegated outside `spec/`, C5 process voice, C6 misplaced content); an independent opus verifier then tried to refute every finding of each report; a cross-chapter contradiction pass over the auditors' claim digests had its own verifier; and the coordinator reviewed every refutation. Only findings whose verification verdict is CONFIRMED or PLAUSIBLE appear, in the verifier's corrected form (lines, scope, class, severity, remedy, dropped citations); 14 refuted findings and 1 refuted negative existential are omitted. Severity is the audit's own grade where it gave one and otherwise follows the rule high = an implementer following the text builds wrong behaviour on a reachable case, medium = ambiguity or a narrower or wider rule that needs another chapter to resolve, low = wording, navigation or placement; every verifier-recommended change is applied and a compound grade such as low-medium is reported at its lower level. Locations are `NN:line` (`map:line` for `SEMANTICS-MAP`), a class marked `was Cn` records a verifier reclassification, and no Kernel message sentence is reproduced. The report holds 212 chapter findings and 6 cross-chapter contradictions.

## Headline conclusions

- The owner's suspicion is confirmed in the larger chapters. spec/07, spec/09, spec/11 and spec/05 are organised as accumulated measured findings: general rules exist but sit as bold lead sentences eroded by later exceptions, and the decisive grammar or table often lives only in Lean or in `docs/sources`. spec/03, spec/04 and spec/10 §3–§4 are the most mechanism-shaped text and survive largely intact.
- The most harmful defect class is the unamended override: a later sentence narrows an earlier one that was never corrected, often with the checklist or a non-normative note still carrying the old rule (examples: 10:168 vs its note and checklist, 11:48 vs 11:50, 09:212 vs 09:144, 12:27's unmeasured 15-digit literal bound vs 04:119). An implementer reading top-down or reading the checklist builds the stale rule.
- In nearly every confirmed contradiction where Lean was checked, Lean already follows one consistent side. The rewrite is therefore mainly a prose task anchored on the Lean definitions named in the findings, not a semantic re-investigation; a small residue (marked unverified below) needs a Kernel measurement first.
- Several decisive tables exist only in Lean: the classification-enabling leaf table (`requiresClassificationPass`), the message-parameter grammar and its check staging, the operator-to-extent-gate mapping for partial validation, and per-operator argument-slot typing. Promoting each into `spec/` closes a recurring class of worker questions.
- Process voice is pervasive: about 600 links to `docs/sources` checkpoints or inbound records sit inside normative sentences, with history narrative ("an earlier version said") and Lean implementation status in places. This is the main cause of the reading cost and should move to provenance, not be deleted.
