# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for working in **a12-kernel-lean** — a clean-room, versioned **Lean 4 formal theory and executable specification** of the A12 kernel's validation & computation semantics.

## What this is

A12 Kernel is mgm technology partners' model-and-DSL engine for complex business forms: business analysts declare **validation rules** (each states the *error* condition — true ⇒ the data is invalid) and **computations** (derived fields) in a bilingual EN/DE DSL, and the engine evaluates them against form *Documents*. This project preserves that observed **evaluation semantics** as a versioned mechanized theory: executable as a reference oracle, empirically anchored to kernel 30.8.1, equipped with a required proof spine plus selectively proved higher-level properties, and designed to produce purpose-specific semantic shipments for independent evaluators and later checked consumers such as importers and rule-refactoring tools.

The project-owned language-neutral semantics live in [`spec/`](spec/) — start at [`spec/SEMANTICS-MAP.md`](spec/SEMANTICS-MAP.md) and follow the numbered deep-dives. They are the semantic bridge to a12-dmkits. [`docs/README.md`](docs/README.md) is the sole registry and ownership map for the living project documentation; do not reproduce its complete catalog here.

- **Extend semantics:** start from the canonical clause in [`spec/`](spec/), use [`docs/SOURCES.md`](docs/SOURCES.md) for its provenance and drill path, record every locally originated behavioral spec change that still needs peer reconciliation in the same change under [`docs/A12-DMKITS-SPEC-SYNC-LEDGER.md`](docs/A12-DMKITS-SPEC-SYNC-LEDGER.md), inspect current clause/proof/evidence coverage in [`docs/IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md`](docs/IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md), inspect or update the selected live obligation in [`docs/SEMANTICS-GAPS.md`](docs/SEMANTICS-GAPS.md), and follow the capsule workflow in [`docs/TESTING.md`](docs/TESTING.md). An inbound correction already committed and reviewed in a12-dmkits is recorded as provenance, not re-issued as an outbound ledger request. [`docs/LEAN-ENCODING-GUIDE.md`](docs/LEAN-ENCODING-GUIDE.md) remains consulted staged guidance, not a writable plan.
- **Resume current work:** read [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md) for the persisted handoff baseline, selected gap or generic route-discovery state, next consumer-probe trigger, and stop conditions; follow its links when a unit was handed off, otherwise follow its ordered checklist and consult [`docs/SEMANTICS-GAPS.md`](docs/SEMANTICS-GAPS.md) for the selected obligation. Keep uninterrupted capsule selection and gate progress in working context instead of rewriting the file.
- **Explore a consumer:** start with the bounded probe catalog in [`docs/USE-CASES.md`](docs/USE-CASES.md), then use [`docs/IMPLEMENTER-GUIDE.md`](docs/IMPLEMENTER-GUIDE.md) for the task profile, probe boundary, semantic shipments, and independent-consumer gates. Use [`docs/PROTOCOL.md`](docs/PROTOCOL.md) only when the selected task actually consumes the normalized reference process.

## Goal & role

**The primary goal is eventual 100% semantic conformance with A12 kernel 30.8.1 across its complete observable validation-and-computation language and required static legality.** Supported fragments are honest intermediate checkpoints, never an acceptable permanent scope ceiling. Reach that target as a versioned mechanized theory, executable-first, with a required proof spine and additional proofs selected by payoff. Build small semantic capsules: each lands with its executable clause, useful law when one exists, nearest checked non-law, exact assumptions, coverage entry, and honest external-evidence status. Exercise closed material through bounded consumer probes across the Execute/Translate/Transform/Compile/Analyze/Verify/Synthesize/Qualify/Explain/Govern taxonomy owned by [`docs/PRODUCT-PROPOSAL.md`](docs/PRODUCT-PROPOSAL.md#general-consumer-task-categories) and explained for users in [`docs/USE-CASES.md`](docs/USE-CASES.md); probes test whether the semantic representation transports and serves real tasks, but never justify leaving kernel behavior unmodeled. Promote only explicitly adopted, research-closed capabilities into versioned purpose-specific semantic shipments. Selected semantic families are differentially calibrated against compact retained observations from the real engine; internally closed families may remain honestly marked `external evidence pending` until a coherent calibration batch. Proofs establish internal universal consequences and semantic-preservation bridges, never universal correspondence to the external kernel or automatic correctness of an independent consumer. Lean is the ecosystem's formal semantics-of-record for the chosen account of observed behaviour—not a replacement for the shipped Kotlin interpreter. Read [`docs/PROJECT-DESIGN.md`](docs/PROJECT-DESIGN.md) for the charter and [`docs/LEAN-FORMALIZATION.md`](docs/LEAN-FORMALIZATION.md) before extending the proof or semantics architecture; read [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) before extending the core types.

## Permanent conformance-convergence rule

**Infer the Kernel's mechanisms and close semantic families. This is the default working method in every session, including after compaction or handoff.** The goal is complete conformance, not a succession of green examples. The [adopted conformance programme](docs/CONFORMANCE-ECONOMY-PROPOSAL.md) owns the finite coverage denominator and host boundaries.

- Select work by the remaining construct/arm coverage and compositions a shared mechanism can close, its demonstrated reuse, and total implementation, measurement, review and integration cost. Replay frequency alone does not set priority: a nearly green population can omit an entire unfinished family.
- Before extending a family, inventory its existing representations and state a compact causal model: name resolution and static admission, operand selection and ordering, phase-specific reads, evaluation/control effects, and observable results. Mark inferred behavior as a hypothesis until the required oracle settles it. Choose discriminators that could falsify the model, including a second composition the motivating case did not supply.
- Use existing source knowledge and retained evidence first. Measure only unresolved distinctions, batched across the independent axes; implement the shared mechanism and instantiate its supported family. A single failing row is evidence about the model, not sufficient justification for another special-purpose carrier. If exceptions accumulate, revisit the model before adding another branch.
- Keep one selected family milestone active per implementation track until its named partitions close or a concrete dependency, falsifying evidence or owner instruction forces reprioritization. Independent tracks require the existing delegation authorization and distinct ownership; this rule grants none. A prerequisite commit does not close the milestone. Require reader-to-evaluator coverage where that route exists, explicit remaining exclusions, and regression preservation; never relabel partial support as broad to meet a deadline.
- Judge progress by verified family/construct closures and unchanged-population gains. Report prerequisites separately. Reuse the existing proportional gates and review triggers; add no dashboard, framework or extra verification loop to enforce this rule. If work keeps producing prerequisites without coverage, stop that sequence and revise its decomposition.

**Standing owner direction, 2026-10-04 — single-track breadth closure.** Follow the [ordered family queue](docs/PLAN.md#single-track-breadth-queue). Work sequentially, with no parallel implementation or review work. Before each batch, check its remaining obligations against the current code, tests, evidence and construct inventory, correcting stale descriptions locally. Implement one shared mechanism and all its ready family members against a finite acceptance matrix, then run one applicable integrated gate and one triggered cold review at the frozen family boundary. Focused red/green and affected-gate reruns after corrections remain required. Candidate coverage gains become reported gains only after verified closure; report broad/partial/absent changes separately from replay gains. Retain the queue order unless a concrete dependency, falsifying evidence or further owner direction requires a change, and explain that change. Reaching all construct/arm cells broadly represented remains a milestone toward complete conformance, not a substitute for closing their remaining compositions.

**Standing owner direction, 2026-10-08 — Having cell closure.** Finish the active Time batch, then enumerate the remaining Having obligations in a concrete closure matrix before selecting further work. Use that matrix to choose larger, settled batches: related operators and compositions share one implementation and applicable gate cycle wherever possible. Judge throughput by verified obligations and construct/arm cells closed, not commits, passing tests or agent activity. Preserve focused red/green checks, required review and honest remaining exclusions; this direction applies specifically to Having. The [resumption plan](docs/PLAN.md#single-track-breadth-queue) owns the next boundary.

**Standing owner direction, 2026-10-10 — fixed good-enough conformance plan.** Execute the frozen C01–C22 [checklist](docs/PLAN.md#ordered-checklist) and its listed acceptance cases, then stop at C22. The target is substantial ordinary Kernel coverage, roughly 99–99.5% as a scope aspiration; the explicit low-risk tail is outside the deliverable. Rarity is not low risk: wrong values, stale reads, swallowed errors, cross-row leaks and silent misadmission take priority even on unusual inputs. Resolve failures of the promised coverage through the existing shared-mechanism tasks. Do not append tasks, expand acceptance cases, add completion gates, park defects in a separate findings/limitations record or begin a follow-on tail programme. Correct the model rather than accumulating example-specific patches. Existing sufficient evidence closes stale tasks without reimplementation. C01/C12 consumer probes test usability; no product launch, downstream adoption or new interpreter/SMT programme is required. PLAN has no word limit; capsule size and gate cadence remain unchanged. Do not claim a measured percentage or universal consumer compatibility without evidence.

## HIGH/MEDIUM task allocation

**Standing owner authorization, 2026-10-06:** use sequential HIGH/MEDIUM delegation for regular, bounded implementation work when the handoff is likely to save total effort. These instructions apply on every resumption and to every delegated agent. Model tiers are symbolic (HIGH, MEDIUM). Resolve them through the active CLI's rows in the tier table in your global instructions; the maintainer's table is [Model tier mappings](https://github.com/mbackschat/coding-setup/blob/main/CLAUDE.md#model-tier-mappings). Keep model selectors and effort values in that table, not duplicated here. Without such a table, run HIGH on your most capable available model and MEDIUM on a faster, cheaper one, and state which models you used. If a table exists but its required configuration is unsupported, report that rather than silently substituting.

- **The main agent is always HIGH:** HIGH coordinates the session and owns final acceptance; MEDIUM serves only as a delegated agent. If the main session does not match the resolved HIGH tier, report the mismatch rather than treating MEDIUM as the coordinator.
- **HIGH owns semantic judgment:** select the family, settle the causal model and oracle, define the finite acceptance matrix and exclusions, and accept the integrated result. Keep unresolved kernel behavior, architecture, ambiguous scope and cross-family decisions at HIGH.
- **MEDIUM implements settled contracts:** suitable tasks include remaining members of an established operator family, localized reader/elaborator wiring, discriminating conformance cases and specializations of existing laws. Delegate a coherent, complete bounded batch whose mechanism and acceptance cases HIGH has settled; keep semantic investigation, unsettled cross-cutting interfaces and tiny edits with HIGH. Before delegating widespread propagation, HIGH settles and checks one representative end-to-end path; warm context does not settle a changed contract. Do not manufacture work or duplicate an implementation to compare tiers.
- **Keep one track and one writer:** HIGH waits while MEDIUM implements; do not overlap implementation, review or build work. MEDIUM must not delegate further. This authorization does not permit independent parallel tracks or weaken any dependency, clean-room or infrastructure approval boundary.
- **Supply a compact handoff:** name owned files, settled mechanism/source basis, acceptance cases, exclusions, narrow build target and stop conditions; preserve existing working-tree changes. Supply verified examples of non-obvious Lean APIs and passing controls for each reader carrier/phase and connective shape, including runtime context, fixture/profile requirements and whether controls affect one or all rows. Admission alone is insufficient; HIGH settles missing base fixtures before delegation. Identify adapters propagating changed context, distinguish raw from already-evaluated inputs, and name refusal locks invalidated by widened APIs. Send task context, not conversation history.
- **Reuse MEDIUM context conservatively:** prefer an idle MEDIUM agent already familiar with the same mechanism and owned files for corrections, remaining family members or a related follow-up under a settled contract. Confirm its tier still matches the global mapping and send the changed contract, intervening edits and current ownership; prior context does not authorize stale assumptions or a wider task. Use a fresh agent when the task is unrelated, intervening changes make its context unreliable, or prior hypotheses could bias a new semantic investigation. Never reuse an implementer or warm reviewer as the required cold reviewer. Reuse is a cost decision, not a quota, and preserves sequential execution.
- **Return uncertainty to HIGH:** MEDIUM stops at a falsified semantic assumption, a required change outside its ownership, or a newly discovered independent mechanism, and returns the smallest reproducer and its evidence. Also return stalled proof or implementation work when successive attempts neither narrow the obstruction nor close an acceptance case. After two consecutive correction runs spent only repairing fixture syntax or admission, return to HIGH to settle the setup before continuing. Include the exact failing obligation, attempted approaches and focused check results. HIGH resolves, takes over or repartitions the task; MEDIUM must not grow its own scope or patch around an unsettled model.
- **Require HIGH review before acceptance:** every MEDIUM change must be reviewed by the coordinating HIGH agent or a designated HIGH reviewer before acceptance, a checkpoint commit or family closure. Batch related MEDIUM tasks into one coherent review when their contract stays settled; review earlier before subsequent work relies on a changed shared interface or semantic assumption. Inspect the actual diff, acceptance-matrix coverage, semantic assumptions and test evidence. For isolated refusal gates, require otherwise-admissible inputs and admitted twins; test competing-defect precedence separately. Apply this equally to HIGH- and MEDIUM-authored checks. MEDIUM's summary or passing builds alone do not establish acceptance. Resolve actionable findings and rerun only invalidated checks before accepting the batch. The coordinating HIGH agent may perform this review directly; no extra reviewer agent is required.
- **Verify proportionally:** MEDIUM performs focused red/green and same-context closure assessment, returning the patch, checks, unresolved issues and live process handles. Batch known compiler/fixture corrections into one focused rerun. HIGH finishes tiny, understood corrections locally when another handoff costs more. Prose-only source edits need no separate build when required integration will compile them. Delegation and HIGH acceptance add no family boundary or gate: integrated, evidence, trust and replay retain their cadence. Never edit compiler inputs while a build is live.
- **Preserve cold-review strength:** for a family led and accepted by HIGH, the implementing session for the existing cold-review tier rule is HIGH, including delegated MEDIUM changes. A triggered cold review therefore remains a fresh, read-only HIGH review of the frozen family slice; ordinary HIGH acceptance of MEDIUM's patch does not substitute for it.
- **Assess economy and self-correct continuously:** at each existing HIGH acceptance step, judge total preparation/context warm-up, elapsed time, build runs, corrections and review against accepted semantic progress. Adjust batch size, stop conditions and reuse from that evidence; keep a task class with HIGH when delegation overhead outweighs benefit. Report tokens only when measured; tier, warm reuse and green checks do not prove savings. Keep routine observations in context, actionable carry-forward decisions in the [handoff](docs/PLAN.md), and durable corrections here. Add no tracking framework or recurring report; report when asked.

## Semantic-first simplification rule

The following permanent semantic-throughput rules govern all future work:

1. Build high-connectivity keystone mechanisms first when their prerequisites and discriminators are ready: the shared checked expression tree and evaluator; reusable resolved operand streams for iteration, filtering, and correlation; computation scheduling and state transition; repeatable addressing and scope resolution; a temporal profile for model-zone calendar behavior; and message construction and interpolation. Once a mechanism is right, close individual operators as small declarations and specialized laws rather than new architectures.

2. Audit related operator families in batches. Produce one semantic decision table, identify the common mechanism and true deviations, implement the common core once, and then instantiate every closed member.

3. Track five completion levels independently: internally executable and tested; important laws and non-laws proved; kernel correspondence retained; public protocol exposed; and consumer-probe qualified. Ordinary semantic work should normally stop after the first two levels until a coherent family milestone justifies later work.

4. Prove generic scan, verdict, observation, poison, selection, and transition laws once at their owning mechanism. Derive operator-specific results by `simp`, rewriting, or specialization wherever their meanings actually agree; do not accumulate theorem restatements distinguished only by an operator name.

5. Reduce conformance to a separating matrix. Every retained case must distinguish a realistic wrong account such as wrong order, empty substitution, polarity, unknown collapse, scope or row selection, or poison timing. Keep one representative per independent branch, and target mutations at semantic seams without creating a mutation framework.

6. Documentation ownership is exclusive. Keep ordinary documentation changes to implemented/evidence state in [`docs/IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md`](docs/IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.md), live semantic obligations in the family shards under [`docs/gaps/`](docs/gaps/), which [`docs/SEMANTICS-GAPS.md`](docs/SEMANTICS-GAPS.md) indexes, and cross-session sequencing in [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md). An untriggered document remains untouched; adding a detailed fact to its owner deletes or replaces displaced copies in the same change. Delete a completed gap instead of retaining shipped history. Change [`docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md) only for a durable non-obvious lesson and the spec-sync ledger only for a canonical semantic correction or a12-dmkits request; change README, architecture, sources, protocol, evidence, or implementer kits only when their owned responsibility changes. [`docs/DOC-DISCIPLINE.md`](docs/DOC-DISCIPLINE.md) defines the trigger and consolidation procedure.

   For human-facing Markdown such as README files and implementer guides, write natural paragraphs without cosmetic fixed-column wrapping. For agent-facing operational owners such as the implementation map, semantic gaps, plan, and source checkpoints, rendered readability is not a goal: choose physical lines and keyed records solely for bounded retrieval, precise mutation, and unambiguous diffs. [`docs/DOC-DISCIPLINE.md`](docs/DOC-DISCIPLINE.md#bounded-detailed-records) owns the exact record contract.

7. Batch external evidence by coherent family. Internally closed clauses may remain honestly marked `external evidence pending`; do not request a kernel capture for each small operator. Follow the [standing population-replay cadence](docs/TESTING.md#population-replay-cadence); a report refresh alone never justifies full replay.

8. **Owner balance, 2026-10-04:** capturing missing semantics is the immediate priority. Use occasional bounded consumer probes at meaningful capability milestones, rather than after every family batch: select a reusable family closure, a major addressing/computation change, or an impending public compatibility claim where the probe can expose a material consumer risk. Between probes, the ordinary consumer hypothesis and same-context adequacy check preserve consumer needs without another independent exercise. A probe tests knowledge transport and does not become a permanent lockstep product suite by default.

9. Interleave breadth with early high-risk spikes. Establish a minimal foundation in each major area, then test its most dangerous uncertainty before broadening routine operators—for example model-zone discontinuities, multi-level outer correlation, scheduling/poison/clearing, and enumeration remapping or locale collisions.

10. Maintain a strict semantic-work ratio: at least three substantial semantic work units must land between infrastructure or product-boundary units. Infrastructure and dependency work must satisfy the approval rules below, including the existing measured-throughput tooling exception; this ratio does not itself authorize such work.

Separate genuine duplication from intentional semantic differences, extract a shared function or theorem only when at least two completed semantic users have the same meaning and result domain, restate member theorems as specializations, and remove a case only after another retained case demonstrably guards the same branch. > **Permanent family batching — all agents:** The [conformance-convergence rule](#permanent-conformance-convergence-rule) applies equally to every agent.

## ⚠️ HARD RULE — clean-room reimplementation; never link, call, or transcribe the kernel

This is a **licensing boundary, not a preference**, and it is the same rule the a12-dmkits interpreter already operates under. The A12 kernel is **EUPL-1.2 (copyleft) OR commercial**. A work that *links, ships, or calls* the kernel is a combined work the EUPL copyleft reaches; a **line-by-line transliteration of its source** is a copyright derivative that re-attracts the EUPL. Therefore:

- **Never** link, call, or ship the kernel (or a JS-kernel binding) from this project, and **never** transcribe a kernel *source expression* into Lean.
- **Do** read the kernel — and the a12-dmkits interpreter under `../a12-rulekit/interpreter/` — to *learn the exact behaviour*, then write **original** Lean and lock its chosen account with focused examples and laws; replay matching retained differentials when available and otherwise record `external evidence pending`. Reading the source to *understand* (and to find surprising special cases) is encouraged; the *fix* is never a transliteration. **Copy the mechanism, never the expression.**

**Message catalogs are protected expression.** Name message keys, codes and parameters; never reproduce rendered catalog sentences in Lean, `spec/`, docs, retained evidence or archived receipts. Describe message classes in original words. [The prose guard](scripts/check-no-kernel-prose.sh) must pass before a commit touching `spec/`, `docs/` or Lean prose.

Because this project's source ships no kernel code, it is licensed **MIT** (see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)) — matching the A12 OSS family (a12-dmkits source is MIT for the same reason; see [`../a12-rulekit/docs/LICENSING.md`](../a12-rulekit/docs/LICENSING.md)).

## ⚠️ HARD RULE — semantics first; harness, governance, and redundant work require explicit approval

A standing instruction to continue, finish, or run autonomously authorizes that semantic sequence; it does **not** authorize a new or expanded harness, process layer, evidence framework, protocol, schema, generator, registry, qualification campaign, migration, documentation-governance reorganization, trust mechanism, or other infrastructure initiative.

## ⚠️ HARD RULE — discharge a claim before stating it, or flag it and surface it

A statement that later probing falsifies is worse than no statement: it enters `spec/`, ships to the peer, and costs a reconciliation round. **Every claim leaving this repository — in `spec/`, a ledger entry, a docstring, a finding, a reply to the user, or a message to a peer session — is either discharged by the method its claim class requires, or explicitly marked unverified.** Confidence, plausibility, and "the peer said so" are not discharge. Prior effort spent reasoning toward a claim is not discharge either. **Marking a ledger entry `accepted` is a claim-leaving act**, not merely a reading: it writes the handback's account into `spec/` and into an immutable receipt in one step. Classify each claim a handback asserts and discharge it by its own class before accepting; a handback that supplies an *outcome* has not discharged a claim about a *mechanism* ([`LF84`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md)).

A peer-session message is the **least gated and furthest-reaching** of those channels: `spec/` meets the synchronization guard, a ledger entry meets the hygiene guard and a record contract, Lean meets the build and the trust audit, and a message meets nothing while the peer quotes it into its own permanent record. So prefer sending a **pointer to a gated record** — entry id, checkpoint anchor, reviewed revision, artifact SHA-256 — over composing a fresh account in prose. A pointer cannot drift from the record because it is the record, and it is the form that still works when no peer is listening. [`LF115`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md) records the session that produced this rule and the measurement behind it. [The peer-coordination guide](docs/TESTING-PEER-COORDINATION.md#peer-session-coordination) owns the coordination protocol, including what to do when no peer is reachable.

Classify the claim, then apply its method. Doing this costs seconds; every skipped instance so far has cost a round trip.

| claim class | example | what discharges it |
|---|---|---|
| **value** — this input yields that output | `2^32` narrows to `0` | an executable case, written **red first**, in the owning conformance module |
| **mechanism** — the output happens *because* of this step | "the parse step causes the asymmetry" | independent re-derivation in a second implementation, **or** a witness that isolates the named cause from its neighbours |
| **reachability / scope** — no legal model expresses this; this gate constrains that | "unauthorable"; "the 15-digit bound gates literals" | **measurement only.** Reading never discharges it. Absent a measurement, do not state it |
| **provenance** — the peer or a source established this | "measured at `<rev>`" | confirm that revision measured *this* case, not an adjacent one that merely shares an outcome |

**Reusing a mechanism on a new carrier is a claim, not an inheritance.** An existing function compiles wherever its types fit; what was measured about it is narrower, and the call site shows nothing. Before crossing an arm, carrier, phase, depth, or topology boundary with a settled mechanism, name the boundary and ask whether the two sides are already known to differ anywhere — a known divergence proves the boundary is real. Then either cite the measurement that covers the new carrier or state the assumption in the owning record with an evidence item beside it. Never silently, because an assumption that happens to hold produces no signal at all ([`LF116`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md)).

Four cheap checks, each of which would have prevented a recorded defect:

- **Never write a 40-character revision you did not read.** Extending a short form into a plausible full SHA produces a citation that resolves nowhere while reading exactly like a measurement receipt. Cite the short form you actually saw, or read the full one off the repository. This is not the rewrite hazard the receipt-continuity note (`docs/archived/A12-DMKITS-SPEC-SYNC-LEDGER-THROUGH-2026-08-28.md#receipt-continuity` at `a050f150`) tracks: that one is external and unavoidable, this one is self-inflicted and free to block.

- **Before consuming *or writing* an unreachability, exclusivity, or *unmeasured-assumption* claim, grep this repository's own `spec/`, `docs/sources/`, `docs/EVIDENCE.md`, and `docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md` for the mechanism it excludes — searching for the literal values a settling observation would carry, not for the words that describe the question ([`LF121`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md)). **`docs/sources/` is the exception, and it is searched heading-first like the peer's:** its checkpoints lead with a `####` line stating the whole claim with its oracle and date, so `grep -n '^#### ' docs/sources/*.md` answers which one *owns* a question, where a value grep only finds whichever body happens to quote that value ([`LF175`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md)). The last two owners are not optional: the map and the checkpoints record coverage and probes, while a finding records a *conclusion*, and a premise is a conclusion ([`LF131`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md)). **A premise on which work is scheduled is itself a claim** — a discriminator written as *X should do A while Y does B* asserts A and B — so classify and discharge it before spending the measurement, not after. "Unrepresentable", "no legal model", "cannot be read off", and "only this mechanism produces that" are one class: a negative existential that falls to a single defeater, and the cheapest place to find one is the prose this project already owns. See [`LF75`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md) and [`LF84`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md). **The fifth corpus is the peer's [`KERNEL-FINDINGS.md`](../a12-rulekit/docs/KERNEL-FINDINGS.md), searched heading-first by the technique the redundancy rule above gives.** It is the largest body of kernel measurements either project holds, and a defeater from it kills a negative existential exactly as a local one does — so search it when the claim is *written*, not only when an entry is sent. Framing that search as redundancy-only once let a false claim reach `spec/` and cost a peer round; [`LF172`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md) records it.
- **Never write "unauthorable", "unrepresentable", "no legal model can express", or a tool's equivalent such as "no verb sets it".** The claim class follows the logical form — a negative existential over a surface — not whether the subject is the kernel or an instrument ([`LF120`](docs/LEAN-FINDINGS.md)). Before writing that a tool cannot do something, enumerate its subcommands and read the `--help` of each one that could own the setting; absent that, state the denominator actually searched. Write `authorable, witness X, measured at <rev>` or `no witness known as of <rev>`. The second claims nothing and so invites no correction; that phrasing difference is the whole ping-pong.
- **Never let reachability gate a semantic clause.** State the total function unconditionally and keep authorability as a separate annotation allowed to be unknown. A reimplementer needs the function either way, and the coupling is what turns every reachability correction into a semantic rewrite.

**When a claim cannot be discharged, it is never silently softened into hedged prose.** Choose explicitly, and say which was chosen:

1. **Drop it** — the unit does not need the claim. Preferred.
2. **Record it as a gap** — the answer does not change any dependent work. Add it to its owning document or the ledger with the discriminator named, and continue.
3. **Escalate it as a blocker** — the answer changes the representation, a proof statement, or dependent work. **Stop and ask the user.** Do not pick an account and proceed. State which account you would choose and why, so the user reviews a judgment instead of supplying one; recommending is not the same as proceeding, and a blocker raised as a bare list of accounts moves the analysis back to the user. File it in the same change so the question outlives the session: an `EXP-` entry when a kernel observation would settle it, or [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md)'s blocker list when an owner decision would, with `Blocked on:` set on the owning gap. A blocker that exists only in a reply is lost when the session ends.

The gap/blocker test is exactly whether dependent work differs by the answer; when that is itself unclear, treat it as a blocker and ask. An unverified claim that reaches the user must be **visibly flagged in the response**, not buried in a qualifier, so the user can decide whether the measurement is worth its cost.

## The source-of-truth hierarchy

Three layers, in authority order for a semantic question:

1. **`../a12-kernel` — the ultimate source of truth (the behavioural oracle).** The real engine. When the local theory and the engine disagree, **the engine wins and the theory is corrected**. Use it to learn and to differentially test — always under the clean-room rule above.
2. **`spec/` and the Lean theory (this repo) — the primary working reference.** The distilled, language-neutral semantics, executable definitions, and proved consequences of the chosen account. The `§n` taxonomy mirrors a12-dmkits, so section numbers line up across repos.
3. **a12-dmkits — the knowledge source, checked out locally as `../a12-rulekit/`.** A peer **clean-room** reimplementation of the same semantics (in Kotlin), a reusable test corpus, semantics ledgers, and an evaluation harness. Its public release is **dmtool-release**.
